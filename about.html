About Mega-Net Software

We build software that runs real operations — not pitch decks.

Mega-Net Software is a Nigerian software company. Mega-Net EHR Cloud is our flagship product — built for the clinics and hospitals that actually use it every day.

How we got here

Built by engineers who stayed close to the problem.

Mega-Net Software started by building operational systems for organisations that needed more than off-the-shelf software could offer — systems that had to be configured by the people actually running the business, not by a vendor on a support ticket queue.

Mega-Net EHR Cloud grew out of that same approach, applied to healthcare: patient management, billing, pharmacy, laboratory, and reporting, brought into one platform that a hospital's own staff can run day to day — plus Financial Sentinel, built specifically because billing anomalies in healthcare settings are common, costly, and usually caught too late.

We're still a small, focused team. That's deliberate — it means the people who built the platform are also the people who answer your support messages.

How we work

A few things we don't compromise on.

🧩

Configurable, not custom-coded

Your staff should be able to add a ward, a tariff, or a role without waiting on us. Software that requires a developer for every small change doesn't scale with your facility.

🔐

Data isolation by default

Every client's data is separated at the database level. Not a promise in a privacy policy — enforced in the database itself.

📞

Support that's actually reachable

In-app tickets and a direct WhatsApp line. If something's wrong, you shouldn't need to find a phone number buried in an old email.

Beyond EHR Cloud

Mega-Net EHR Cloud is our flagship. It isn't the only thing we build.

Mega-Net Software also builds HospitalPro, Revenue Guard, PharmacyPro, a Clinic Appointment System, ChurchPro, an ERP system, and runs Mega-Net Academy — for organisations with different needs than a full hospital platform.
