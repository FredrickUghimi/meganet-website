Mega-Net Academy

Software is only as good as the staff who run it.

Mega-Net Academy trains your team to actually use Mega-Net EHR Cloud well — not just log in, but run your facility on it with confidence from week one.

Talk to Us About Training
Why this exists

The system doesn't fail. Untrained staff using it does.

Most EHR rollouts don't struggle because the software is wrong — they struggle because front-desk staff, nurses, pharmacists, and billing officers were never properly trained on the specific workflows they'll use every day.

🎯

Role-based, not generic

A registration officer and a lab technician don't need the same training. Academy sessions are built around what each role actually does in the system.

🧑‍🏫

Hands-on, not slides

Staff train inside the real platform on realistic scenarios — patient intake, billing, discharge — not a slideshow about the platform.

🔁

Ongoing, not one-off

New hires join facilities constantly. Academy supports refresher sessions and onboarding for new staff, not just a single launch-day training.

What's covered

Training tracks by role.

Front Desk

Registration & Appointments

Patient intake, demographics, scheduling, and appointment management from day one.

Clinical

EMR & Patient Records
