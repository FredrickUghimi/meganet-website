What It Does

See the screens your team will actually use

Real screens from Mega-Net ERP Cloud. Tap any screen to see it larger.

Dashboard

Know how your business is doing, at a glance.

Today’s sales, the money owed to you and the money you owe, your cash and bank balances, and profit so far, all on one screen.

Follow-ups that are due and products running low on stock show up too, so nothing waits until you go looking.

New sale

Record a sale in seconds.

Pick the customer, add the items, enter the amount received and press Record Sale.

You can tag the sale to a project as you go. The books are updated for you.

Invoice

Professional invoices, ready to send.

Each invoice shows the customer, the project, the total, what has been paid and the balance still due, with the issue and due dates.

Download it as a PDF to send to your customer.

Receivables

See who owes you, and for how long.

Every customer’s outstanding balance, grouped by how long it has been owed: 0 to 30 days, 31 to 60, 61 to 90 and over 90.

On a phone, the report turns into easy-to-read cards, one per customer. Payables works the same way for what you owe suppliers.

Banking and statement

Your real bank accounts, always up to date.

Add your bank accounts and cash in hand, enter each opening balance, and see your total cash and bank position.

Balances move as you record sales, purchases and expenses. Each account has a statement of money in, money out and the running balance.

Profit and Loss

Are you making money? One page tells you.

Income against expenses for the period you choose, with every expense category listed and your net profit at the bottom.

No month-end spreadsheet needed. The report is ready whenever you open it.

CRM

Never lose a lead again.

Mark customers as Prospect, Contacted, Negotiating, Won or Lost, and see how many you have at each stage.

Set a follow-up date and a Follow-Ups Due list shows who to call next, with overdue ones flagged. Keep notes on each customer.

Projects

Track every project’s income and costs.

Tag sales, purchases and expenses to a job, site or contract as you record them.

Each project shows its revenue, costs, profit, margin and how much of the budget is used.

Announcements

Keep your whole team informed.

Post a notice to everyone, or only to selected roles. Pin the important ones and set an expiry date.

Staff see an unread count, and you can see who has read each one. Announcements are plain text and stay inside the app. Nothing is sent by email or SMS.

HR and Payroll

Run payroll and give staff their own payslips.

Keep employee records and leave requests, then create the month’s payroll for everyone at once. PAYE tax, pension and other deductions are worked out from rules you can adjust, and each person gets a payslip PDF.

Staff can log in to see and download their own payslips. Salaries are visible only to the Owner and Admin.

The payroll rules are a starting point. Please have your accountant check them before you rely on the figures.

The Accounting Engine

A full set of books, built automatically

Every sale, purchase, payment, and expense above quietly becomes a proper accounting entry underneath — a Chart of Accounts, Journal Entries, and a General Ledger, kept current without anyone manually posting a single line.

Chart of Accounts

Every account your business needs, structured and ready — no setup expertise required.

Journal Entries

Generated automatically from ordinary actions — recording a sale or paying a supplier, not a separate bookkeeping step. When an accountant needs a correction, they can post a manual entry; every entry is marked manual or automatic, and recorded in the audit trail.

General Ledger

Always up to date, always reconciled — the single source of truth behind every report the system shows you.

Financial Reports

Sixteen reports, from Profit & Loss to the payroll filing schedules — generated on demand, not compiled by hand at month-end. Every one is listed below.

All 16 reports

Financial statements

  • Profit & LossIncome and expenses for a period, and the bottom line.
  • Balance SheetWhat the business owns, owes, and is worth as of a date.
  • Cash FlowCash in and out for a period, and the opening/closing balance.
  • Trial BalanceEvery account's balance as of a date, checking that debits equal credits.

Ledger & adjustments

  • General LedgerDetailed transaction history and running balance per account.
  • Journal EntriesPost manual debit/credit adjustments to the chart of accounts, and reverse them.

Payroll filing

  • PAYE ScheduleEach employee's PAYE for a month's approved payroll, for filing with the tax authority.
  • Pension ScheduleEach employee's and the employer's pension contributions for a month, for remitting to the PFA.

Money owed

  • ReceivablesWhat customers currently owe, aged by how long it has been outstanding.
  • PayablesWhat the business currently owes suppliers, aged the same way.
  • Customer StatementsOne customer's sales, payments and credit notes for a period, with a running balance.
  • Supplier StatementsOne supplier's purchases, payments and credit notes for a period, with a running balance.

Business activity

  • Sales ReportTotal, count and average sale for a period, by day, week, month or customer.
  • Purchase ReportTotal, count and average purchase for a period, by day, week, month or supplier.
  • Expense ReportSpending for a period, broken down by expense category.
  • Inventory ReportCurrent stock levels, stock value, and what is running low.

The difference: the accounting engine runs underneath everything, automatically — you never have to open a "journal entry" screen to make it work.

AI Assistant

Ask your business a question, in plain language

"What were today's sales?" "Who owes us the most?" "Why did profit fall this month?" The AI Assistant answers from your own records, using the same reports the rest of the system runs on — and shows you the figures it used.

It only sees what the person asking is already allowed to see, it can't create or change any record, and every question is kept in the audit trail.

The Differentiator

Business Sentinel — a second pair of eyes on your books

Built-in checks that flag what's worth a second look: cash movements with no matching transaction, credit sales with no invoice or receipt, journal entries that are unusually large for your business, overdue customers who've never made a payment, and stock counts that don't match their movement history. Run a scan whenever you like; findings stay on the list until someone reviews them.

Same idea as Financial Sentinel on Mega-Net EHR Cloud, applied to general business operations instead of hospital billing.
