The differentiator

Ordinary EHRs record what happened. Financial Sentinel tells you what's wrong while it's still happening.

  • Rules-based anomaly detection scans billing activity continuously — not just at month-end reconciliation.
  • Every flag comes with a plain-language explanation, not a raw log entry your team has to decode.
  • The system detects and explains. Your team decides what to do — Sentinel never auto-reverses a charge on its own.
  • Built directly into billing, so there's no separate audit tool to license, install, or reconcile against.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does implementation take?

Timelines depend on facility size and how much historical data needs migrating. A single clinic can be live in days; a full hospital rollout is discussed during your demo.

Can we migrate our existing patient records?

Yes — migration approach depends on your current system's data format. We'll assess this during onboarding.

Does it work without a stable internet connection?

Mega-Net EHR Cloud is cloud-native. For facilities with a self-managed, offline-first setup, ask about Mega-Net HospitalPro instead.

How is pricing structured?

Per facility, based on scope (clinic, hospital, or network) — not per user. Reach out for a quote tailored to your setup.

What does Financial Sentinel actually flag?

Unusual billing patterns — like charges outside a ward's normal range, or activity that doesn't match typical patient flow — each with a plain-language explanation for your finance team to review.

Is support included?

Yes — in-app support tickets and a direct WhatsApp line are part of every plan.
